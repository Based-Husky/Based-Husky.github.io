FAQ

How does $BHUSKY help dogs?

$BHUSKY is the token that brings our community together. There is no tax on trades, so buying or selling does not send money to the charity wallet: donations come from the community feeding the bowl, from our products and from the BHUSKY team. Every donation is public, with its onchain proof.

Where do the donations go?

Into our charity wallet on Base. Every time it reaches $200, we donate it to a dog rescue through Endaoment, rotating between five rescues. Each donation goes on our Impact page with its transaction.

How can I donate?

On our Donate page: pay in USDC with Base Pay in a few seconds, or send USDC on Base from any wallet.

Is the contract safe?

No one can promise that about any token, but you can check the facts yourself: no mint function, no tax, ownership renounced and the original liquidity locked forever. Each point in Huskenomics links to BaseScan. Only buy what you can afford to lose.

Are donations tax-deductible?

No. The charity wallet is managed by the BHUSKY team, so donations to it are not tax-deductible.
